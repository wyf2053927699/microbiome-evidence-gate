# Figure 4 readiness: evidence-aware axis prioritisation

Date: 2026-08-17

## Decision

Figure 4 is analytically complete and has been assembled as a **No-Go
prioritisation figure**, not as a validated mechanism network. None of the four
candidate families passes the eight essential project gates. The PDF/TIFF/PNG
and fallback SVG exports are present; the editable-text PDF is the vector master.

## Completed inputs

- project-measured eight-dimension gate matrix;
- edge-level provenance for ten external original-research edges;
- explicit separation of project-measured and external evidence;
- four prespecified weighting schemes (equal, measured-layer emphasis,
  bridge emphasis and validation emphasis);
- rank-stability output and promotion status.

## Main result

The bile-acid family ranks first under all four weighting schemes, aromatic/AHR
second, choline/acyl-choline third and indole/AHR fourth. These ranks are
exploratory only. All candidates remain `NO-GO` because weighting cannot replace
a failed essential gate. The bile-acid family retains strong longitudinal human
metabolite evidence, but lacks a reproduced upstream microbial function, a
reproduced axis-specific bulk host programme, donor-aware single-cell
localisation and resolution of contradictory evidence.

## Implemented Figure 4 panels

1. eight-dimension measured-evidence heatmap;
2. provenance-coded external edge map using distinct line types for direct
   experimental, human association and project-unmeasured evidence;
3. four-scheme rank/score sensitivity panel;
4. contradiction and missing-evidence ledger;
5. explicit `NO VALIDATED AXIS` decision box.

No biological arrow may be drawn as project-validated merely because an
external paper supports the same class-level relationship.
