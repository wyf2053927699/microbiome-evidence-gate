# Claim boundaries

- CRC discovery and validation are partitions of one participant cohort. They are leakage-controlled internal validation, not independent population replication.
- GSE200997 is analysed as donor-by-condition whole-atlas pseudobulk. The supplied annotation does not establish validated cell types; the analysis therefore does not support cell-type localisation.
- The framework benchmark evaluates specification conformance and simulated coding-error trade-offs. It is not a biological truth, clinical-accuracy or universal-portability benchmark.
- Literature and database edges are priors unless directly measured in an eligible project dataset.
- A missing or failed essential gate cannot be compensated by positive evidence elsewhere.
